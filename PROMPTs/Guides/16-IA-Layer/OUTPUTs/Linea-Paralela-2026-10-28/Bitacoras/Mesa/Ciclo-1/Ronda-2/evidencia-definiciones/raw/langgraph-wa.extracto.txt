Copy page This guide reviews common workflow and agent patterns. 
 Workflows have predetermined code paths and are designed to operate in a certain order. 
 Agents are dynamic and define their own processes and tool usage. 
 LangGraph offers several benefits when building agents and workflows, including persistence , streaming , and support for debugging as well as deployment . 
 Trace and compare these workflow patterns with LangSmith . Follow the tracing quickstart to see how data flows through each step. We recommend you also set up LangSmith Engine which monitors your traces, detects issues, and proposes fixes. 
 Agents are typically implemented as an LLM performing actions using tools . They operate in continuous feedback loops, and are used in situations where problems and solutions are unpredictable. Agents have more autonomy than workflows, and can make decisions about the tools they use and how to 
solve problems. You can still define the available toolset and guidelines for how agents behave.
